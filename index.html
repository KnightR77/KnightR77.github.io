About

Research focus and academic background.

My work focuses on practical sensing systems that move from hardware to human impact. I am interested in wearable and embedded devices that produce actionable feedback in sports, health, and ubiquitous computing settings. Across projects, I work on system design, prototyping, firmware, signal processing, and end-to-end evaluation.
